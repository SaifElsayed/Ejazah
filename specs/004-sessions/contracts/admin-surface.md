# Contract: The administrator surface (Phase 3)

Mounted on `adminRouter` (research R-318). Every procedure is an `adminProcedure`: a signed-in administrator with the
second factor (NFR-SEC-6, FR-480); every call writes one audit entry with the actor, the action, the target's
non-personal label (ADR 0021 D2), the instant and, for every action, the mandatory reason; reads included, as Phase 2's
monitor does. Every procedure declares `.output(schema)`. Nothing here computes, stores or moves an amount, or confirms
an enrollment (SC-413): `sessions.restore` returns an enrollment's status to `confirmed` and never writes `confirmed_at`
(FR-455, R-HONEST-1). Every procedure here is rate-limited (spec FR-470): `disputes.revise` and `sessions.restore` by
their own declared key, every other one by `rate_limit_signed_in_default` (seeded at 120 until T062 confirms the final value before B36: the larger of 120 and research R-325's heaviest normal minute × 2–3, never a number above 200 (R-321, item 5)), the administrator as subject (kernel.md,
"Rate limits").

|---|---|---|---|---|

| `cases.resolve` | `{ caseId, note }` | `{ caseId, status: 'resolved' }` | `case.resolved` | A complaint answers `NOT_FOUND` here and in `cases.dismiss`: it is decided by `cases.decideComplaint` only. `resolution = compensation_ordered`; the note says what Platform Administration did outside the platform.
