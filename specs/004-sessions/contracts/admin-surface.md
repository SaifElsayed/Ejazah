# Contract: The administrator surface (Phase 3)

Mounted on `adminRouter` (research R-318). Every procedure is an `adminProcedure`: a signed-in administrator with the
second factor (NFR-SEC-6, FR-480); every call writes one audit entry with the actor, the action, the target's
non-personal label (ADR 0021 D2), the instant and, for every action, the mandatory reason; reads included, as Phase 2's
monitor does. Every procedure declares `.output(schema)`. Nothing here computes, stores or moves an amount, or confirms
an enrollment (SC-413): `sessions.restore` returns an enrollment's status to `confirmed` and never writes `confirmed_at`
(FR-455, R-HONEST-1). Every procedure here is rate-limited (spec FR-470): `disputes.revise`, `sessions.restore`, `sessions.review.revise` and `cases.openManual` by
their own declared key, the two name searches `sessions.monitor.list` and `users.list` by Phase 1's `rate_limit_search`
(60 per 60 s, failing closed; the owner's decision of 2026-11-06 on the fourth analysis, C2), every other one by `rate_limit_signed_in_default` (150 per 60 s per research R-325, the owner's decision of 2026-11-06 on the fifth analysis, the panel's heaviest normal minute, 45 × 3 = 135, under it with no value of its own), the administrator as subject (kernel.md,
"Rate limits").

**The audit target** (the owner's decision of 2026-11-06 on the fifth analysis, G3; T002). An action on one row
targets that row, by its type and its id: `session` (a `sessions` row), `enrollment` (an `enrollments` row),
`dispute` (a `disputes` row of any kind, a session dispute, a compensation case or a complaint, so every
`dispute.*`, `case.*` and `complaint.*` action and the held files' two) and `provider_outage` (a `provider_outages`
row), the four types T002 adds; a person's standing and record keep Phase 1's `user`. A list read (`*.listed`)
targets the administrator who read it (`admin`, their id), as Phase 1's `*.read` and Phase 2's `payments.listed` do.
A row below names its target where the action's name does not.

**The audit actions' names** (the owner's decision of 2026-11-06 on the sixth analysis, M3; T002). `/audit` and the
home name each action this phase records by the catalogue's text, never the stored code: `ACTION_TEXT` in
`apps/admin/src/pages/audit/actions.ts` maps each to an `admin.audit.actions.*` key of `apps/admin/src/i18n/ar.json`
and `en.json`, in the panel's existing wording (a short verb phrase, as «إيقاف معلم», "Suspend teacher", and
«عرض المدفوعات», "View payments"). Thirty-eight actions, every one in the tables below:

`RemedyMonitor` = `{ remedyId, kind, status, offeredAt, choiceClosesAt, proposal?: { localDate, startTimeLocal, timezone, startsAt }, counter?: { localDate, startTimeLocal, timezone, startsAt, proposedAt, answerClosesAt }, caseId?, decidedAt?, bookingRetriedUntil? }` (R-323, GAP-4): `answerClosesAt` computed when read (data-model §2.3, D1); `bookingRetriedUntil`, derived and never stored, for a remedy `proposed` or `awaiting_choice` past `choiceClosesAt`, its booking refused and retried (FR-468, A3): the proposal's start less the remedy's `snapshot_first_session_lead_hours`, after which it goes to a `makeup_refused` case; shown «booking refused, retried until …». An open remedy carries the action that opens `cases.openManual` with its `remedyId`. The proposal is the enrollment's slot's current local start (catalog's slot) on `proposed_date`, in the enrollment's snapshot zone (the owner's decision of 2026-11-10 on B37 lane B's Q6).

The monitor's other shapes (T024; the owner's decision of 2026-11-10 on B37 lane B's Q5):

- `nextSession` (a list item's) = `{ sessionId, localDate, startTimeLocal, timezone, startsAt }`: the earliest `scheduled` session not yet over.
- `enrollment` (`sessions.monitor.get`'s) = `{ enrollmentId, student: Party, teacher: Party, status, track, sessionCount, completed, remaining, teacherNoShows, repeatedAbsence, restorableUntil? }`: a list item's fields without `nextSession`. `restorableUntil` = the closing case's `created_at` plus `enrollment_restore_window_days` as days of 24 hours (Q7), present for an enrollment `terminated_no_show` with an open `absence_termination` case, or `cancelled` whose last cancellation's reason is `admin` with an open `student_fault_confirmed` case, whether or not it has passed.
- `makeupFor` = `{ sessionId, sequenceNumber }`: the session a make-up makes up for.
- `cases` = `[{ caseId, reason, status }]`: the enrollment's compensation cases only, newest first, `reason` the closed code; a complaint of a teacher's absence is `/cases`'.
- `cancellationRequests` = `[{ requestId, sessionId, requestedBy: 'student' \| 'teacher', reason, status, createdAt, respondedAt? }]`: the requests of the enrollment's sessions, newest first, `status` as the row records it.
- `events`: at one instant the `session_events` rows come before the `enrollment_events` rows, each source in its own order.
- A name search (`student`, `teacher`) reads `EnrollmentSide.enrollmentPage` in batches, each twice the one before, from `admin_list_page_size` and one more, until the page holds one more match than it shows or the rows end, as `payments.list` does (Q3).
- `payments.get`'s `holds` (below) is a required array, empty for an enrollment with none.

`Telemetry` = `{ sessionId, schedule: { localDate, startTimeLocal, timezone, startsAt, endsAt }, status, events: [{ party: 'student' \| 'teacher' \| 'room', type: 'joined' \| 'left' \| 'room_ended', occurredAt, connectionRef? }], intervals: { student, teacher, together }, simultaneousSeconds, joined: { student, teacher }, outages: [...] }` (FR-SESS-9).
