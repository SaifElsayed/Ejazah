# Feature Specification: Phase 4 — Certification, reports and notifications (lightened)

**Feature Branch**: `Ejazah_app/004-sessions/T057` (the cycle, `specs/004-sessions/tasks.md` T057); the phase's batches then run as `Ejazah_app/005-certification/<task ids>`

**Status**: Clarified (2026-11-16)

**Input**: User description: "Phase 4 of PLAN.md, lightened by the owner's plan of 2026-11-15 and SRS DEC-70: the certificate (the teacher's request, the administrator's issue, the Arabic PDF with the chain), user reports and the gender rules, push for three kinds only, the settings surface (specs/005-certification), T057"

## Overview

**Objective, from PLAN.md**: grant the certificate that is the product, and close the gender rules and the user reports
that the MVP cannot launch without.

Phase 3 left a teacher and a student meeting in sessions that settle from evidence. Phase 4 lets the teacher who holds
an approved credential for a subject ask the platform to issue an ijazah to their student; an administrator reviews the
request beside the filled certificate and issues it, or rejects it with a reason. The certificate is generated by the
platform only (ADR 0013), in Arabic or English as the teacher chooses, with the credential's chain and the granting
teacher appended, frozen at issue (FR-ISNAD-7), and the teacher's stored signature; it renders a chain of any length
(ADR 0004, ADR 0017 D4). An administrator may revoke an issued ijazah with a reason and may bar a user from receiving
ijazahs. Any user may report another from a session, a chat or a profile, and may flag another user's declared gender;
administrators work both queues, correcting a gender declaration (with its consequences on enrollments) or banning for
fraud. The platform sends push notifications for three kinds only in this release: a session's reminder, a
cancellation request and its answer, and a payment's result (DEC-70 as amended by DEC-71); each is recorded, queued and retried, in the user's language. The
administrator's settings page exposes every setting of SRS §3.14 without a deployment.

**Who builds it, and how big it is.** Track 1 of the owner's two tracks (PLAN.md, «How a phase is run», as amended on
2026-11-16), one implementer pair with Saif's review of each batch, from 11/16 to 11/19, beside track 2 (the money) on
its own test database. The lighter gates of 2026-11-15 apply: full TDD for security, gender and time; lighter specs for
ordinary screens.

**What the first update (v1.1) delivers instead** (SRS DEC-70; PLAN.md, «The first update (v1.1)»): ratings and
reviews (FR-REVIEW-1…5, FR-ADMIN-11), the public verification page and the certificate's QR code (FR-IJAZAH-10;
FR-IJAZAH-8's verification endpoint), the notification centre (FR-NOTIF-2), and every other push kind of FR-NOTIF-1.
Their tasks are written in this phase's tasks.md under «v1.1», untimed. No requirement is removed; this specification
requires none of them.

**Scope boundary.** This phase delivers: the teacher's signature (FR-IJAZAH-12); the certificate name (ADR 0019 D1);
the ijazah request, its review and issue (FR-IJAZAH-1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 11, 15; FR-ISNAD-7; FR-ADMIN-12); revocation and the
bar (FR-IJAZAH-8, 9); user reports and their queue (FR-SESS-15, FR-ADMIN-15); gender flags, their queue, the correction
and the fraud ban (FR-GENDER-3…7, FR-ADMIN-8); push for three kinds (FR-NOTIF-1 as amended, FR-NOTIF-3, FR-NOTIF-4,
FR-BOOK-8); the settings page (FR-ADMIN-9, §3.14). It moves no money (ADR 0007): no part of a certificate, a report or
a gender decision computes, holds or moves an amount; a compensation case a gender decision opens is Phase 3's
`openCompensationCase`, which writes no amount. A control whose action belongs to v1.1 is absent from the screens,
never shown disabled (R-MOBILE-13).

**Given, not re-specified.** The credential, its review and its transcribed chain (FR-ISNAD-1…5, FR-ADMIN-19, Phase 1);
the student's and teacher's standing through `StandingService`; Phase 3's enrollment closings, compensation cases and
the restore (FR-CANCEL-8 as amended by DEC-69: an enrollment a gender correction ended is never restored); the
caller kinds, guards, settings, audit log, upload port and content sniffing, `packages/time`, the live channel; the
`ijazahs`, `ijazah_bars`, `gender_flags`, `user_reports`, `notifications`, `teacher_signatures` and
`device_push_tokens` tables the init migration holds (ERD §8, §10, §11).

**Reviewed by this cycle** (`specs/004-sessions/tasks.md` T057): the Not-scheduled row of `specs/004-sessions/tasks.md`
(item 3: the cancellation request is the first thing that needs push — met by FR-619); the Not-scheduled row of

## User Scenarios & Testing _(mandatory)_

### User Story 1 - A teacher requests an ijazah and an administrator issues it (Priority: P1)

A teacher with an ijazah-track enrollment and an approved credential for its subject uploads a signature once, then
sends a request with the completion date and the certificate's language, after seeing the certificate as it will be
filled. If the student has no certificate name, the request waits and the student is asked for the name to print,
once. An administrator sees the request beside the same filled certificate and issues it, or rejects it with a reason
the teacher sees. Once issued, the student and the teacher can view and download the certificate at any time, and it
stays on the student's record.

**Why this priority**: The certificate is the product (PLAN.md, Phase 4's objective).

**Independent Test**: With a seeded approved credential and an ijazah-track enrollment, send a request without a
signature (refused), with one (pending), approve it as an administrator, and download the PDF as both parties; reject
a second request and read the reason as the teacher.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a teacher with no signature image, **When** they try to request an ijazah, **Then** the request is refused and they are sent to upload one.
2. **Given** a teacher without an approved credential for the enrollment's subject, or a recitation enrollment, or a barred student, **When** they request, **Then** it is refused with its cause and nothing is written.
3. **Given** a student with no certificate name, **When** the teacher requests, **Then** the request waits as «awaiting the student's name», the student is asked for the name once, and the request goes on to the administrators once it is entered.
4. **Given** a pending request, **When** an administrator approves it, **Then** one ijazah is issued with a unique certificate number, the chain snapshot with the teacher appended, the printed names and both dates in Hijri and Gregorian; the request is approved and audited; the PDF follows from the worker, and the parties see it as being prepared until an administrator signs off on it.
5. **Given** a pending request, **When** an administrator rejects it with a reason, **Then** no ijazah exists and the teacher sees the reason.
6. **Given** a signed-off ijazah, **When** the student or the granting teacher opens it, **Then** the PDF opens through a link that expires; nobody else can open it.
7. **Given** a rendered certificate not yet signed off, **When** an administrator opens it, **Then** they sign it off and both parties can open it, or they have it rendered again (after a correction of the chain or the name, which re-freezes it), and nobody else sees it meanwhile.

---

### User Story 2 - A certificate renders right whatever the chain (Priority: P1)

The certificate is correct Arabic (shaping, ligatures, embedded fonts) or English, and a chain of any length fits:
up to 60 generations on one A4 page, longer chains on a following page; a generation of several names is drawn as one
row.

**Why this priority**: A certificate that renders wrong is worse than none (FR-IJAZAH-11).

**Independent Test**: Issue certificates for chains of 1, 25, 60 and 61 generations, one with a multi-name generation,
in both languages, and inspect each PDF.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a 60-generation chain, **When** issued, **Then** the certificate is one A4 page with every name readable.
2. **Given** a 61-generation chain, **When** issued, **Then** it continues on a second page in list form, nothing cut.
3. **Given** a name containing markup or template characters, **When** issued, **Then** it prints as text.

---

### User Story 3 - An administrator revokes an ijazah or bars a user (Priority: P2)

An administrator revokes an issued ijazah with a mandatory reason; it is never deleted, it is marked revoked for both
parties, and it leaves the student's public credentials. Revocation bars nothing: barring a user from receiving
ijazahs is its own action with its own reason, and can be lifted.

**Why this priority**: The platform must be able to withdraw its attestation (FR-IJAZAH-8, FR-IJAZAH-9).

**Independent Test**: Revoke an ijazah, then send a new request for the same student (allowed); bar the student (new
requests refused), lift the bar (allowed again).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an issued ijazah, **When** revoked with a reason, **Then** both parties see it marked revoked, the audit records the reason, and its PDF is served to nobody, the parties included (R-HONEST-6; the owner's answer D1 (a), SRS DEC-72).
2. **Given** a revoked ijazah, **When** the teacher requests again for that student, **Then** the request is accepted.
3. **Given** a barred student, **When** any teacher requests for them, **Then** the request is refused; **When** the bar is lifted, **Then** requests are accepted again.

---

### User Story 4 - A user reports another, and an administrator acts (Priority: P2)

From a session, a chat or a profile, a user reports another with a reason. The report reaches the administrators'
reports queue, where an administrator marks it actioned or dismissed with a note, and may move to the tools that
already exist (a standing action, a gender flag, an identity-document request).

**Why this priority**: The SRS requires a report path from every session and profile (FR-SESS-15) before launch.

**Independent Test**: Report from each context; see each in the queue; action one and dismiss one.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a participant of a session, **When** they report the other with a reason, **Then** an open report exists with its context and the session, and the reporter cannot see what the administrator decides beyond «received».
2. **Given** a user who has no enrollment with the reported user, **When** they report from a profile they can see, **Then** the report is accepted; **When** they report a profile they cannot see (another gender's teacher), **Then** it answers as not found.
3. **Given** an open report on a session, **Then** that enrollment's chat is kept while it is open (FR-492, Phase 3).

---

### User Story 5 - A gender declaration is disputed, corrected, or found fraudulent (Priority: P1)

Any user, or an administrator acting on a report, flags another user's declared gender. Administrators review the flag
and may require identity documents. They dismiss it, correct the declaration (an honest error: every enrollment the
correction makes non-compliant ends, its remaining sessions cancelled by the administrator, both parties told, a
compensation case per enrollment), or find fraud (the account is banned, all its enrollments cancelled, a compensation
case for each counterparty enrollment). Nobody changes their own declared gender.

**Why this priority**: Gender separation is the platform's first rule (R-GENDER); without the correction path an error
can only be fixed in the database.

**Independent Test**: Flag a student with two enrollments; correct the gender; both enrollments end with cases and
cannot be restored. Flag a teacher; find fraud; the teacher is banned and every counterparty enrollment has a case.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an open flag, **When** an administrator corrects the declaration, **Then** the user's gender changes, every enrollment now pairing two genders ends (remaining sessions Cancelled-By-Admin), one compensation case per enrollment opens, and no ban is written.
2. **Given** an open flag, **When** an administrator finds fraud, **Then** the account is banned through `StandingService`, all its enrollments are cancelled with cases for the counterparties, and the lift of that ban checks each pairing first (DEC-69).
3. **Given** any user, **When** they try to change their own declared gender, **Then** no path exists.
4. **Given** a correction, **When** any participant then calls any procedure of the ended enrollments, **Then** the adversarial suite's «after an administrator's gender correction» case holds.

---

### User Story 6 - Three kinds of push arrive in the user's language (Priority: P2)

A user with the app installed receives a push before each session (at the configured offsets, 24 hours and 15 minutes
by default), when the other party asks to cancel a session or answers their own request to cancel, and when their payment is confirmed, failed or expired.
Each push is recorded, sent by a queued job with retries, in the user's language, and tapping it opens the screen
it is about.

**Why this priority**: A cancellation request expires at the session's start, so a counterparty whose app is closed
would never learn of it in time (`specs/004-sessions/tasks.md`, Not scheduled, item 3); reminders are FR-BOOK-8.

**Independent Test**: With the push provider's stand-in, book a session, request a cancellation, pay and fail a
payment; see each notification row and each delivery, a failed delivery retried, and the reminders moved when the
session's instants are recomputed.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a session, **When** its start minus an offset passes, **Then** both parties get one reminder each, never twice, and none for a session cancelled before.
2. **Given** a session whose instants are recomputed (ADR 0009 D3), **Then** its reminders follow the new start.
3. **Given** the push provider down, **When** a notification is due, **Then** the request that produced it still succeeds and the delivery is retried.
4. **Given** a user who chose English, **Then** the push is in English.

---

### User Story 7 - An administrator changes a setting without a deployment (Priority: P3)

The administrators' settings page lists every setting of SRS §3.14 with its current value, its default and what it
governs; an administrator changes one within its allowed range, with a reason, and the change is audited and takes
effect without a deployment.

**Why this priority**: FR-ADMIN-9; required before launch, but no user flow depends on it.

**Independent Test**: Change the chat retention and a rate limit; read the audit entries; see the new values used.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a setting, **When** an administrator saves a value outside its range, **Then** it is refused with the range.
2. **Given** a valid change, **Then** one audit entry records the old and new values and the reason, and readers use the new value within the settings cache's period.

---

### Edge Cases

- A teacher sends a second request for the same enrollment while one is pending: refused.
- The teacher is suspended, revoked or banned while a request is pending: the request stays pending; an administrator may issue it from what was frozen at the request, or reject it with a reason (FR-627, FR-628). The teacher cannot delete their account, and an administrator cannot delete it, while the request is undecided (FR-630).
- The student deletes their account while a request is pending: the request closes, nothing is issued (a pending request does not block the student's deletion, since its enrollment is no longer ongoing); an issued certificate keeps its printed names after a deletion (ADR 0019 D1).
- The credential's chain is corrected after issue: the issued certificate keeps its snapshot (FR-ISNAD-7).
- An administrator corrects a certificate name on the student's request: future certificates print the new name; issued ones keep theirs.
- A gender correction on a user with no enrollment: only the declaration changes.
- A report against oneself, or a duplicate open report by the same reporter on the same user and context: refused.
- A push to a user with no device token: the notification row is written and never pushed (`pushed_at` stays empty); no error.
- A payment confirmed after its order expired (Phase 2's late payment): one push per recorded outcome, never two for one order.

## Requirements _(mandatory)_

### Functional Requirements

**The signature and the certificate name**

- **FR-601**: A teacher MUST be able to upload, view and replace a signature image (an image, content-sniffed, size-limited, stored encrypted and served by expiring signed link); an ijazah request MUST be refused while none exists (FR-IJAZAH-12).
- **FR-602**: A student whose account has no certificate name MUST be asked for it when a teacher's first request for them is sent, shown as it will print, entered once; afterwards only an administrator MAY change it, on the student's request, with a reason, audited (ADR 0019 D1, FR-IJAZAH-15).

**The request and the issue**

- **FR-603**: A teacher MUST be able to request an ijazah from an ijazah-track enrollment of theirs in a subject they hold an approved credential for, giving the completion date and the certificate language (Arabic by default, FR-LOC-4), after seeing the certificate as it will be filled (FR-IJAZAH-1, FR-IJAZAH-15). A request MUST be refused for a teacher who is not active, a recitation enrollment, a barred student, a missing signature or credential, or while another request of that enrollment is pending; each refusal is logged with ids and its reason.
- **FR-604**: An administrator MUST see each pending request with the same filled certificate and MUST approve it or reject it with a mandatory reason the teacher sees; no administrator action MAY start a grant or change a chain (FR-IJAZAH-7, FR-ADMIN-12). Both are audited (FR-ADMIN-17).
- **FR-605**: Approving MUST issue exactly one ijazah per request, idempotently: a unique, non-guessable certificate number (16 Crockford base32 symbols, shown 4-4-4-4), a verification token stored only as its hash (minted now so v1.1's page can verify every certificate), the chain snapshot with the granting teacher appended (FR-ISNAD-7), the printed student and teacher names (ADR 0019 D1), the completion and issue dates in Hijri and Gregorian, and the teacher's signature; the PDF follows from the worker, and until an administrator signs it off (FR-629) the parties see the certificate as being prepared (R-API-13); mode is always platform-generated (FR-IJAZAH-2, ADR 0013).
- **FR-606**: The certificate MUST render correct Arabic and English, a chain of any length (one A4 page up to 60 generations, a following page beyond), multi-name generations as one row, and every name as text, never markup (FR-IJAZAH-11; ADR 0004 D1–D3; ADR 0017 D4). It is rendered outside the request, in the worker.
- **FR-607**: The student and the granting teacher MUST be able to view and download a signed-off certificate at any time through an expiring signed link; nobody else (FR-IJAZAH-3, FR-IJAZAH-10's link rule). Every signed-off ijazah MUST appear on the student's record as a permanent credential (FR-IJAZAH-4).
- **FR-608**: In v1.0 the parties MUST learn of an issue or a rejection on screen: the request's status on the enrollment for the teacher and the student (pending; issued, with the certificate; rejected, with the reason, to the teacher), refreshed by the live channel while the app is open (FR-427). The issue's and the rejection's push arrive in v1.1 (FR-IJAZAH-15's «notifying both parties», SRS DEC-70; the owner's answer of 2026-11-16).

**Revocation and the bar**

- **FR-609**: An administrator MUST be able to revoke an issued ijazah with a mandatory reason; it is never deleted, both parties see it revoked, the student's profile stops showing it, its PDF is served to nobody, the parties included, and the audit records the reason (FR-IJAZAH-8, R-HONEST-6, SRS DEC-72). Revocation MUST NOT bar future requests (FR-IJAZAH-9).
- **FR-610**: An administrator MUST be able to bar a user from receiving ijazahs, and lift the bar, each with a reason, with history kept; a barred student's requests are refused (FR-IJAZAH-9, FR-ADMIN-12).

**Reports**

- **FR-611**: A signed-in user MUST be able to report another user with a reason from a session they are a party of, from a chat of theirs, or from a profile they can see; one open report per reporter, reported user and context; reporting oneself is refused; the reporter sees only that it was received (FR-SESS-15).
- **FR-612**: Administrators MUST have a reports queue listing open reports with context and evidence links, and MUST mark each actioned or dismissed with a note, audited (FR-ADMIN-15). An open report on a session keeps that enrollment's chat (FR-492).
- **FR-613**: Report creation and gender-flag creation MUST each be rate limited per user by a declared setting: reports 5 per 24 hours, flags 3 per 24 hours (§3.14 as amended by DEC-71); the first refusal in a window is logged with ids.

**Gender flags and decisions**

- **FR-614**: Any signed-in user MUST be able to flag the declared gender of a user they can see or are paired with; an administrator MUST be able to raise one, from a report or on their own; one open flag per flagged user (FR-GENDER-3).
- **FR-615**: Administrators MUST have a gender-flag queue; a flag is dismissed, corrected or resolved as fraud, each with a mandatory reason, audited; an administrator MAY require identity documents first (FR-GENDER-4, FR-VERIFY-10, FR-ADMIN-8).
- **FR-616**: A correction (an honest error) MUST change the declaration, end every enrollment the new gender makes non-compliant (remaining sessions Cancelled-By-Admin), open one compensation case per ended enrollment, write no ban, and leave those enrollments never restorable (FR-GENDER-7; FR-CANCEL-8 as amended by DEC-69).
- **FR-617**: A fraud finding MUST ban the account through `StandingService`, cancel all its enrollments with a compensation case per counterparty enrollment; the ban's lift checks each pairing first (FR-GENDER-6, DEC-69).
- **FR-618**: No user MAY change their own declared gender by any path (FR-GENDER-5).

**Push for three kinds**

- **FR-619**: The platform MUST record a notification and push it for exactly three kinds in v1.0: `session_reminder` to both parties at each configured offset before a session (FR-BOOK-8; `reminder_offsets_minutes`, 24 hours and 15 minutes by default), the cancellation request and its answer, `cancellation_requested` to the other party of a session and `cancellation_request_accepted` or `cancellation_request_declined` to the party who asked (FR-CANCEL-1…3), and the payment's result to the student (`payment_confirmed`, `payment_failed`, `payment_expired`, read from Phase 2's durable records, PLAN.md) (SRS DEC-70 as amended by DEC-71). An expired request (`cancellation_request_expired`) sends no push in v1.0.
- **FR-620**: Delivery MUST be queued and retried, never sent inside a request, so a push provider outage fails no booking (FR-NOTIF-4); each notification is pushed at most once per device; a recorded delivery time on the row.
- **FR-621**: A push MUST be in the recipient's language (FR-NOTIF-3), carry no name, amount or free text beyond its keys and ids, and open the screen it is about.
- **FR-622**: Reminders MUST follow a session's instants when they are recomputed (ADR 0009 D3) and MUST NOT be sent for a session no longer scheduled.
- **FR-623**: A user MUST be able to register and remove the device's push token; a signed-out or deleted user's tokens are removed.

**A request outlives the teacher's standing (the owner's answer of 2026-11-16, C3)**

- **FR-627**: When a request is sent, the teacher's signature image, printed name (the credential's holder name) and the credential's chain MUST be frozen onto the request; the certificate is issued from what the request froze, never from the teacher's live data. A request MAY be sent only by a teacher who is active.
- **FR-628**: A request sent while the teacher was active MUST stay pending whatever happens to the teacher afterwards (a suspension, a ban or a revocation). An administrator MAY issue it or reject it with a reason, and MUST see the teacher's current standing and its reason beside it. A teacher's deletion cannot happen while it is undecided (FR-630).

**The sign-off, the deletion and the signature (the owner's answers of 2026-11-16, SRS DEC-72)**

- **FR-629**: A rendered certificate MUST NOT be shown to either party until an administrator has opened the rendered PDF and signed it off; the administrator MAY instead have it rendered again. Until the sign-off, an administrator's correction of the credential's chain or of the student's certificate name MUST re-freeze the issued record and render it again. The sign-off and each re-render are audited and idempotent.
- **FR-630**: A teacher's own deletion (in the app and on the web) MUST be refused while one of their ijazah requests is undecided (awaiting the student's name, pending, or approved and not yet signed off), as an ongoing enrollment refuses it. An administrator's deletion of a teacher MUST be refused while the teacher has an enrollment awaiting payment or confirmed (one paused by a suspension included) or such a request; the administrator first ends each enrollment with the direct end and decides each request. Each refusal writes nothing and is logged with ids and its cause. An administrator's deletion of a student is unchanged.
- **FR-631**: A deleted teacher who was ever approved MUST keep their signature image with the documents SRS DEC-67 keeps, read and erased under the same rules (FR-ADMIN-21).

### Key Entities _(include if feature involves data)_
