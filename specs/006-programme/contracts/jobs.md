# Jobs: the programme

Each job follows the jobs README: identifiers-only payloads; settings read on every run; a double-run spec through
`runOnceAndTwice` (R-API-14).

| Job | Kind | Schedule | What it does | Idempotent by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `advance-programmes` | sweep | every minute | only programmes whose enrollment is `confirmed` and that have no open `teacher_suspended` or `student_suspended` case (a suspension pauses, FR-SESS-16; R-508), each under its row lock, one transaction each (research R-508): (1) `awaiting_offer` past `offer_due_at`, or session 1 ended with no make-up pending 24 h ago → the `no_offer` item, once per deadline (never a lapse); `offered` past `pay_by` unpaid → `offer_lapsed`; (2) the next month due at the paid month's last session's end (`lastToEnd`), and with a saved card an automatic `instalment` order recorded `created`; (3) a soft decline retried at 1, 3, 5, 7 days, at most 4; (4) the pause at the next occurrence, with a live `enrollment` signal. After commit: `chargeSavedCard` once for each order just recorded; an order left `created` with no gateway id is never called again and expires, and its expiry with no answer counts as a soft decline that starts the retries (R-509); in live mode a charge with no payee is refused and recorded as a failure. One log line of ids and a reason per action, no card detail | each step reads the state it moves from; an order `created` and unexpired is never recorded twice |
| `confirm-enrollment` (changed) | on demand | — | per order, by `payments.confirmed_at`: `assessment` → session 1 and `awaiting_offer`; `rest_in_full` → sessions 2..N+1; `instalment` m → its sessions from max+1 with `programme_month` m, `paused_at` cleared, an open stop request withdrawn. Expected kinds skip the cover and case gates; a second paid order for a paid month → `duplicate_payment`. A refused generation never cancels a programme: the order stays paid, a case opens | `payments.confirmed_at` |
| `release-teacher-shares` (changed) | sweep | hourly at :35 | the `assessment` milestone (not continued), the programme's half on the share of everything paid with the assessment counted, `ijazah_end` and `programme_stopped` (R-512); sessions valued by their own order | `@@unique([enrollmentId, milestone])` |
| `reencrypt-columns` (changed) | sweep | as built | covers `programme_cards.token_encrypted` | as built |
