1. Who we are and how to contact us

The Ijazah app is a platform connecting students of the Holy Quran with their teachers. The person responsible for your personal data (the data controller) is Khaled Nasser, a natural person, of Dreamland, 6th of October City, Giza, Egypt. He is also the data protection officer.

For any question or request about your data, write to: support@ijazahapp.com

2. In short

  • We collect only what the service needs: your account, your bookings and sessions, your chats, and a teacher's documents to verify them.
  • Audio and video in sessions pass live between the two parties and are not recorded.
  • We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • Our servers are in France, and we use service providers whose names and locations section 8 lists.
  • You can delete your account from the app at any time; section 11 says what is deleted, what is kept and why.
  • If you are under 18, you use the app with your parent's or guardian's agreement.
  • You have rights over your data, which we answer free of charge within 6 working days.

3. The data we collect

From every user:

  • Your display name, your e-mail address, and your Google account ID if you sign in with it.
  • Your gender, date of birth, time zone, preferred language, and your photo if you add one.
  • Your device's notification token.
  • The app's build number on your device, and crash reports when the app fails (without your name or e-mail; they carry your account ID only).
  • Technical connection data, including the IP address, to protect the service from abuse. The server's logs keep only the IP address's network, not the full address, for 90 days.

From a student:

  • Bookings and packages, and payment status and records (amount, date and status).
  • If you choose to save your card for an ijazah programme's automatic payments: a reference from Paymob and the card's last four digits and brand. The full card number never reaches us.
  • Chats with the teacher, attendance times in sessions, the reports and complaints you make, and your ratings.
  • The name written on the ijazah certificate.

From a teacher:

  • The identity document (national ID card or passport).
  • The credential (ijazah) documents and their text (the riwaya, the tariq and the chain), and the legal name on them.
  • The verification call's notes, and the history of decisions on the application and the credentials.
  • The payout account reference, and the signature image that appears on certificates.
  • The bio, qualifications, subjects, packages and available times.

In sessions: audio and video pass live between the two parties and are not recorded. We record only the connection and attendance times.

For anyone under 18: we record the time the box «My parent or guardian agrees to my registration» was ticked.

4. Where we get it from

  • From you, when you sign up and use the app.
  • From Google, if you sign in with it: your name, e-mail address and account ID.
  • From Paymob: the payment's status, and the card's last four digits and brand if you choose to save it.
  • From your use of the app: session and connection times, and crash reports.

6. Sensitive data

Some of what we collect is sensitive data under the law: financial data (the payout account reference and payment details), and all data of anyone under 18. We ask your consent to process it at sign-up, with one box: «I agree to the Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy», without which sign-up does not finish.

7. Minors and guardians

Sign-up is open to every age. If you are under 18, you use the app with your parent's or guardian's agreement: sign-up asks one more box, without which it does not finish, «My parent or guardian agrees to my registration»; we record the time it was ticked, and the administration sees that the account is a minor's.

We build no profiles of minors, do not track them and show them no advertising. Sessions are always between a teacher and a student of the same gender.

8. Who sees your data

The other party: the teacher and the student of the same enrollment each see the other's display name and photo, if any, the chat between them and the session times. A student sees an approved teacher's profile: the bio, the credentials, the chain and the rating.

Platform administration: authorised administrators see what they need to review teachers' applications and to decide complaints and reports. A chat is read only inside a complaint or report open on that enrollment.

9. Transfers outside Egypt

Our servers are in France (the European Union), and we use global service providers such as Google, whose data may be processed in the United States. These transfers are needed to provide the service. The places: France for the server, the European Union for files and crash reports, the United States for e-mail, and Egypt for payment, as the table in section 8 shows.

The data kept about an approved teacher after their account's deletion 5 years from the deletion (the next paragraph)

If a teacher who was once approved deletes their account, we keep their identity documents, credential documents, payout account details and signature image, together with the legal name on their credentials, the credentials' text, the verification notes and the history of decisions on their application and credentials. We keep them for one purpose only: to prevent fraud and protect students' and the platform's rights, so that no one escapes responsibility by deleting an account. They are used for nothing else, are opened only by an authorised administrator with a recorded reason, and are kept for 5 years from the account's deletion, then erased, the decision records staying without the name. An applicant who was never approved keeps nothing after deletion, and an identity document on a live account is erased 90 days after its decision, except an approved teacher's.

11. Deleting your account

In the app: open «Account», then «Delete account», type the confirmation word, then confirm it is you by signing in again. The deletion is immediate and final, and cannot be undone.

Without the app: from the account deletion page on our website (/delete-account), by entering the account's e-mail address and then a code we send to it.

What is deleted: your name, e-mail address, date of birth and photo, your chats and the comments on your ratings, your sign-in sessions and notification tokens, and your stored files.

What is kept, and why: payment, session and enrollment records without your personal data, as the law requires; issued ijazahs, which stay valid; star ratings; and what an open complaint or report needs, until it closes. A teacher once approved keeps what the last paragraph of section 10 says.

When an account cannot be deleted: an account with an ongoing enrollment, as a student or a teacher, cannot be deleted: an enrollment awaiting payment, confirmed (even if paused), or ended by the account's own ban or revocation while its time is still held. Nor can a teacher's account with an undecided ijazah request. You can delete it once these end, or write to support at support@ijazahapp.com to end them; the administration then deletes the account at your request.

Your deleted data leaves the backups within 14 days.

12. Your rights

You have the rights over your data that the law sets out:

  • To know about it, access it and obtain it.
  • To withdraw your consent.
  • To correct, modify, delete, add to or update it.
  • To restrict its processing to a specific scope.
  • To be notified of any breach of it.
  • To object to its processing where it conflicts with your fundamental rights and freedoms.

Ask for any of them by writing to support@ijazahapp.com. A request is free of charge, and we answer it within 6 working days. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Centre (pdpc.gov.eg).

13. How we protect your data

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data: we limit access to those who need it for their work, open documents through short-lived temporary links, record every access to sensitive documents and its reason, and keep regular backups.

14. If a breach happens

If your data is breached, we notify the Personal Data Protection Centre within 72 hours of learning of it, then notify you within 3 working days of notifying the Centre, by e-mail or by a notification in the app.

15. Messages and notifications

We send you messages and notifications about the service only: sign-in codes, bookings, session reminders, and the outcome of applications and payments. We send no marketing messages, and will send them only with your prior consent and a way to stop them.

16. Cookies and tools

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tool; the only tool is crash reporting (Sentry). The public pages of our website, this one included, set no cookies.

17. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a material way, we tell you before the change takes effect, by e-mail or by a notification in the app. We record every version and its date below.
