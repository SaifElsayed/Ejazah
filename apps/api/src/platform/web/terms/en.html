A technology intermediary

The platform is a technology intermediary connecting a student with a teacher and is not a party to the teaching agreement between them. The teacher is responsible for the session's content and timing; the student for attending.

Payment

The student pays the package price to the teacher directly through the payment gateway. The platform holds no funds and takes no commission on sessions; it records the payment's status and issues the notice.

Compensation

If the teacher misses a confirmed session, the student is entitled to a make-up session at a time both agree within the period the platform sets, or to a refund of that session when a make-up is not possible.

If the student misses a session without cancelling within the allowed time, the session counts and is not refunded. The cancellation and compensation rule in force is shown at booking. Any reimbursement is decided by Platform Administration through the moderation workflow.

Identity and gender

The user confirms the accuracy of the details given at sign-up, the declared gender included. Sessions take place between a teacher and a student of the same gender. The platform may require identity documentation at any time, and falsifying gender is grounds for banning the account.

Conduct

The user behaves respectfully in sessions and conversations. The platform may suspend or ban an account that breaches these terms or harms another user.

Amounts paid for packages in progress are not refunded when an account is suspended or banned for a breach of these terms.

The platform notifies users before any change to these terms, and the new version is accepted at the next sign-in.
