# booking

## Owns

`slot_holds, enrollments, enrollment_events`

Listed in `packages/config/module-tables.json`. Reaching a table outside this list fails the build citing R-API-6.

## Never

Write the `sessions` table; an enrollment's sessions are written by the sessions module's `generateForEnrollment`, which the `confirm-enrollment` job calls on the confirmation's transaction, and booking reads sessions only through that module's entry (R-API-6, ADR 0005 D2). Reference a wallet, ledger, balance, escrow, or a gateway amount (gross, fee, net) (R-MONEY-6, `boundaries/no-money-vocabulary`); the only money booking carries is a package's price and the enrollment's snapshot of it, as `Piasters` (`specs/003-booking/plan.md`, Constraints). Set `confirmed_at` anywhere but `confirmEnrollment`, called only by the `confirm-enrollment` job (`apps/api/src/jobs/confirm-enrollment.ts`, payments' job in ARCHITECTURE §4) on a persisted, signature-verified paid event; no client, teacher or admin path exists (R-HONEST-1, FR-PAYG-8). Read a `payments` row or import the payments module: what booking needs of the orders, their standing, is handed in as payments' `orderStandingsOf` (R-API-6, R-208). Hold a slot anywhere but the `slot_holds` table (ADR 0005 D4).

## Locks

Every function of the entry that writes takes the caller's transaction and says in its comment which locks it takes or expects. They follow T005's one order (`apps/api/src/platform/db/row-lock.ts` and the notes under T005 in `specs/003-booking/tasks.md`): `users` (both accounts in one `lockRows` call), `teacher_profiles`, `lockKey('teacher-slots')`, `availability_slots`, `slot_holds`, `enrollments`, then `payments` (through the `orders` the caller hands in), rows of one table in id order; each row is locked before what is decided on it is read again (rule 1), and a path that inserts a row referencing `users` locks that account first. `placeHold`, `placeCheckoutHold`, `enrollOnHold`, `lockOrderTarget`, `releaseHolds`, `reholdEnrollment` and `lockForConfirmation` take their locks in that order; `recordEnrollmentEvents` and `recordOrderFailed` take none and expect the enrollment's (and the payment's when the row names one). The reads for discovery, the booking sheet, the lists, the checkout's reads, the re-resolution's lengths and the payments monitor take no lock: nothing is decided on them.

## Shape

The rules live in the services (`*.service.ts`), the queries in the repositories (`*.repository.ts`), and each router is thin: validate, call one service method, return (`booking.router.ts` under `booking`, `discovery.router.ts` under `discovery`, `enrollments.router.ts` under `enrollments`, `teacher-students.router.ts` under `teacher.students` and `teacher.today`). Booking imports identity, catalog and sessions (ARCHITECTURE §4, the module graph); payments, the jobs and `main.ts` import it. `index.ts` is the only import path.

`catalog-use.repository.ts` (T020) answers catalog's `CatalogUse` port, bound in `main.ts` through `createCatalogUse`: whether a package has an enrollment, and the enrollment count of each package; whether a slot is in use is `slot-occupancy.service.ts`'s since T026. Queries only, on the caller's transaction and with no lock: the caller holds its locks before it asks (T005's rule 1).

`slot-occupancy.service.ts` and `slot-occupancy.repository.ts` (T026) are a slot's occupancy (R-205): taken by an active hold or an enrollment `pending_payment`; held by a `confirmed` enrollment on every local date before its release, the week after its final session (read through the sessions module's entry) plus `slot_release_buffer_weeks`. `nearest-slot.service.ts` is the discovery card's nearest free slot per teacher of a page, by `firstOccurrenceAfter` with `first_session_lead_hours`, over catalog's offered slots and identity's predicate with `purpose: 'book'`. `discovery.service.ts` composes catalog's discovery service (the `DiscoveryCatalog` seam) with it, and `discovery.router.ts` serves `discovery.browse` and `discovery.filters` to a student under `rate_limit_search`. Read-only: no lock.

`booking-slots.service.ts` (T032) is the booking sheet's slots of one package: the package read through catalog's entry for its teacher alone, identity's predicate with `purpose: 'book'` asked of that teacher before anything else (NOT_FOUND otherwise), catalog's offered slots of exactly the package's length, free by the occupancy above on their first session's date, less those any of whose sessions overlaps the student's or the teacher's own sessions (`session-overlap.service.ts`, the one check the hold applies too; read through the sessions module's entry, T033); each with its first session by `firstOccurrenceAfter` and its last by `resolveOccurrences`, the calls generation makes. `booking.router.ts` serves `booking.slots` to a student under `rate_limit_search`. Read-only: no lock, nothing written.

`holds.service.ts` and `holds.repository.ts` (T033) are holds, functions of the entry on the caller's transaction: `placeHold` (FR-351 to FR-353: the predicate, the student's account and the slot decided after both accounts and the slot are locked; the slot's and the student's expired holds released first; the insert for `slot_hold_ttl_minutes` plus `slot_hold_grace_minutes`; every one of the package's sessions checked against both parties' own; a refusal thrown, so the caller keeps nothing), `releaseHolds` and `releaseExpiredHolds` (FR-363: the holds, then their enrollments, then payments' order standing, each locked in id order; an enrollment awaiting payment cancelled with its reason and one event row, one with a `paid` order left awaiting payment except under `slot_unavailable`), and `reholdEnrollment` (FR-PAYG-19, for the confirm job). The order standing is payments' `orderStandingsOf`, handed in by the caller: booking reads no `payments` row and does not import payments. `enrollment-events.repository.ts` is booking's one writer of `enrollment_events` rows (R-216).

`confirmation.service.ts` and `confirmation.repository.ts` (T039) are the `confirm-enrollment` job's booking, on its transaction: `lockForConfirmation` takes every lock in T005's order (both accounts, the slot, the active holds on the slot and of the student, their enrollments with this one, then payments' orders through the `orders` handed in) and reads the enrollment under them; `confirmEnrollment` converts the enrollment's hold by a conditional update and only then confirms it from `pending_payment` or `cancelled`, with one `confirmed` row naming the order, the only writer of `confirmed_at` (R-HONEST-1); `refuseEnrollment` releases what it holds through `releaseHolds` under `slot_unavailable` and cancels it, `slot_unavailable` or, refused for a party while already cancelled, its own reason, with one `cancelled` row (FR-362).

`hold-expiry.repository.ts` (T040) is what the expiry sweeps read, with no lock: a page of the expired active holds with no enrollment (`expire-holds`) and with one (`expire-pending-payments`), each released through `releaseHolds` under `expired`, which locks before it decides; and, of some enrollments, those `confirm-enrollment` may still take (awaiting payment or cancelled), for the sweep's hand-off of a paid order.

`termination.subscriber.ts` (T041) is booking's consequence of an account's end, subscribed to identity's `AccountConsequences` by `main.ts` over payments' `orderStandingsOf`: on the termination's transaction, after its account lock, it takes the teacher's slots' key, reads the active holds on the account's slots and the account's own (`activeHoldIds` in `holds.repository.ts`) and releases them through `releaseHolds`, `teacher_suspended` or `admin` (`account_deleted` for a deletion), so their enrollments awaiting payment are cancelled with one `enrollment_events` row each and one with a paid order is left for the confirm job. It writes no row referencing an account.

`enrollment-lists.service.ts` and `enrollment-lists.repository.ts` (T049) are the lists: the student's `enrollments.list` and `enrollments.get` (`enrollments.router.ts`), the teacher's `teacher.students.list`, `teacher.students.get` and `teacher.today` (`teacher-students.router.ts`), whose services are part of `BookingServices`. Every enrollment is read by the caller's own id, so another person's answers `NOT_FOUND`; the active tab (`pending_payment` and `confirmed` for the student, `confirmed` for the teacher) is ordered by next session in one page, the ended tab (`completed`, `terminated_no_show`, `cancelled` after confirmation) paged by enrollment id; a checkout cancelled before confirmation is on neither. Sessions, their progress (upcoming, completed, remaining: `sessionProgress`) and the teacher's day are read through the sessions module's entry, names through identity's `displayNamesOf`, the teacher's search through the kernel's `idsWithFoldedNamePrefix` over the tab's own students. The teacher sees a student's display name and nothing else of them. Read-only: no lock.

`checkout-holds.service.ts` and `checkout-holds.repository.ts` (T036) are what payments' checkout asks of booking (R-208): `placeCheckoutHold` (`checkout.hold`, FR-BOOK-12: the student's own unexpired hold released with `checkout_abandoned`, a `paid` order on it refusing the new checkout, or `PayableCheckout` thrown while an order on it is still payable, so the caller cancels it at the gateway outside any transaction and starts again; then `placeHold` in the same transaction), and `retryTargetOf` and `lockOrderTarget` (`checkout.retry`, FR-356: the enrollment and its hold read with no lock before the gateway, then locked and read again). `pay.service.ts` and `pay.repository.ts` (T036) are `checkout.pay`'s part: `payTargetOf` (the student's own hold and its package compared with what the student saw, `SUMMARY_CHANGED`, before the gateway, with no lock) and `enrollOnHold` (under the locks, the enrollment written with the id the caller drew, its snapshot and the quoted first session's date, and the hold linked by a conditional update). `checkout-reads.service.ts` (T037) is `checkout.current`'s and `checkout.status`'s reads: the student's own unexpired hold with its package's summary now and the first and last session it would get, and the student's own enrollment as the database holds it, with its sessions once generated.

`order-failed.repository.ts` (T038) is the `order_failed` outcome payments' order branch appends through `recordOrderFailed`, written by `enrollment-events.repository.ts`. `session-lengths.repository.ts` (T048) is each enrollment's session length for the time zone re-resolution job. `order-enrollments.service.ts` and `order-enrollments.repository.ts` (T052) are what the admin's payments monitor reads of an order's enrollment: its student and status for a page of orders, and one enrollment's package, status, progress and outcomes; payments reads them here and never reads `enrollments` itself.

## Requirement ids

- FR-312, FR-318 (a package with an enrollment and a slot in use keep their terms; R-207): `catalog-use.repository.ts`, `slot-occupancy.service.ts`.
- FR-394, FR-BOOK-10 (a slot offered again from the week after its final session plus the buffer; R-205): `slot-occupancy.service.ts`.
- FR-335 (the card's nearest free slot; R-205): `nearest-slot.service.ts`, `discovery.service.ts`, `discovery.router.ts`.
- FR-365, FR-385 (a failed order's `order_failed` outcome, appended by the payments module's order branch on its transaction; R-214, R-216): `order-failed.repository.ts`, which writes through `enrollment-events.repository.ts`, booking's one writer of `enrollment_events` (B29's integration).
- FR-350, FR-386 (the booking sheet's slots and their first and last sessions; R-205, R-209): `booking-slots.service.ts`, `booking.router.ts`; the same previews of the held slot on `checkout.current` (T085): `checkout-reads.service.ts` through `sessionPreviewsOn`.
- FR-351 to FR-353, FR-363, FR-PAYG-19, FR-BOOK-5 (holds, their releases and the one overlap check; R-208): `holds.service.ts`, `holds.repository.ts`, `session-overlap.service.ts`.
- FR-385 (the outcomes' rows; R-216): `enrollment-events.repository.ts`.
- R-219, FR-PAYG-6, FR-BOOK-9 (an account's end releases its holds and cancels their enrollments awaiting payment): `termination.subscriber.ts`.
- FR-361, FR-362, FR-375, FR-PAYG-19 (the confirmation and the refusal of a paid order, for `confirm-enrollment`; R-215): `confirmation.service.ts`, `confirmation.repository.ts`.
- FR-390 to FR-393, FR-395, FR-BOOK-7 (the student's enrollments, the teacher's students and day, derived progress, both zones, the fields each side sees): `enrollment-lists.service.ts`, `enrollment-lists.repository.ts`, `enrollments.router.ts`, `teacher-students.router.ts`.
- FR-BOOK-12, FR-354, FR-356, FR-358 (booking's part of `checkout.hold`, `checkout.pay` and `checkout.retry`; R-208): `checkout-holds.service.ts`, `checkout-holds.repository.ts`, `pay.service.ts`, `pay.repository.ts`.
- FR-380 to FR-386 (`checkout.current`'s and `checkout.status`'s reads; T037): `checkout-reads.service.ts`.
- FR-370 (each enrollment's session length for the time zone re-resolution; R-210, T048): `session-lengths.repository.ts`.
- FR-400, R-220 (what the admin's payments monitor reads of an order's enrollment: its student and status for a page of orders, and one enrollment's package, status, progress and outcomes; read-only, no lock): `order-enrollments.service.ts`, `order-enrollments.repository.ts`.
