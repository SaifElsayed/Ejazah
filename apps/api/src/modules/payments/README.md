# payments

## Owns

`payments, payment_events`

Listed in `packages/config/module-tables.json`. Reaching a table outside this list fails the build citing R-API-6.

## Never

Hold, route, balance, transfer or refund money; the gateway settles directly to the teacher and the platform takes 0% (FR-PAYG-1, FR-PAYG-10). Compute a net amount; record what the gateway reported (R-MONEY-5). Act on an event before its signature is verified on the raw body (R-MONEY-9) and its provider event id is persisted under the unique NOT NULL column (R-MONEY-8). Confirm an enrollment from a client redirect, a manual toggle, an admin action or an upload; the only path is the persisted paid event and the idempotent `confirm-enrollment` job (R-HONEST-1, FR-PAYG-8). Write `enrollments` or `teacher_profiles` directly; go through `booking` and `identity` public entries (R-API-6). Store a raw payout account number; only the gateway's recipient reference, encrypted (R-DB-8).

## Shape

The rules live in the services: `checkout.service.ts` (the student's checkout, T036, T037), `order-events.service.ts` (the webhook's order branch, T038, T056), `recipient-events.service.ts` (the payout recipient's events, T077) and `payments-monitor.service.ts` (the admin's monitor, T052). The queries live in the repositories (`payments.repository.ts`, `checkout.repository.ts`, `order-standing.repository.ts`, `confirmation-order.repository.ts`, `order-expiry.repository.ts`, `payments-monitor.repository.ts`); `__fixtures__/event-rows.repository.ts` is the specs' read-back of `payment_events` and nothing a request runs. Payments imports identity and booking (ARCHITECTURE §4, the module graph); the admin module, the jobs and `main.ts` import it. `index.ts` is the only import path. `checkout.router.ts` is thin (the student's checkout) and `payments-admin.router.ts` is the read-only monitor (T052); payout setup and its status are the identity module's `teacher.payout` (research R-121). `webhook.controller.ts` is the raw-body HTTP endpoint outside tRPC, `POST /webhooks/payment` (T077), verified through `platform/guards/webhook-signature.guard.ts`'s `verifyInboundEvent`; `recipient-events.service.ts` records a signed payout-recipient event and hands its verdict to `identity`'s entry in the same transaction (research R-114, R-121). `order-standing.repository.ts` (T033) answers booking's release of a hold whether an enrollment has a `paid` order and whether its last order failed, locking the orders after the caller's enrollments (FR-363). `confirmation-order.repository.ts` (T039) answers the `confirm-enrollment` job: an order's enrollment, read with no lock, and, under the job's locks, its status and when its first paid event was recorded. `order-expiry.repository.ts` (T040) is the `expire-pending-payments` sweep's: a page of the `created` orders past their expiry and their move to `expired` under the orders' locks, and a page of the `paid` orders whose paid event was recorded before an instant, for the hand-off to `confirm-enrollment`. Jobs: `confirm-enrollment` and `expire-pending-payments` are this module's by ARCHITECTURE §4, and live in `apps/api/src/jobs/`, where they call this module, booking, catalog, identity, moderation and sessions through their entries; the compensation case a refused or duplicate payment opens is the job's call to moderation's `openCompensationCase`, not this module's.

The gateway is reached only through the payment port (`PaymentPort`, `apps/api/src/platform/ports/payment/`): the stand-in (`fake.adapter.ts`) and, since T056, Paymob (`paymob.adapter.ts`), selected by `PAYMENT_GATEWAY` and never named here (R-API-4, R-API-5). The adapters live in the port's folder, not in this module.

## Locks

T005's order (`apps/api/src/platform/db/row-lock.ts`): an order's lock always comes after its enrollment's. The order branch locks the enrollment, then the order, before it reads the order's status again (T038); `heldOrdersOf` and `orderStandingsOf` expect the caller to hold the enrollments and lock their orders after them, in id order in one `lockRows(tx, 'payments', ids)` call; `markOrdersExpired` locks the expired orders before it moves them. The port is called outside any transaction (ARCHITECTURE §8.1). The monitor's reads take no lock.

## Requirement ids

`SRS-PAY-*`: FR-PAYG-1 to FR-PAYG-19, FR-ADMIN-18, section 3.6. _Filled as they land._

- FR-PAYG-4, FR-PAYG-15, FR-191 (Phase 1, T077): the webhook, and the recipient events recorded once and routed to the teacher by the reference's hash.
- FR-360, FR-365, FR-367 (Phase 2, T038; R-214): `order-events.service.ts`, the webhook's order branch. In one transaction, the order's enrollment and then the order locked (T005's order), the event recorded under its unique provider event id with the body's hash, the order moved monotonically (`created` to `paid`, `failed` or `expired`; `failed` or `expired` to `paid`; `paid` to `refunded` only) with the transaction id, fee and net as reported on `paid`, the address cleared, and a failed order's `order_failed` outcome appended through booking's `recordOrderFailed`; after commit, every paid event of a known order, a replay included, enqueues `confirm-enrollment` under the job id `paymentId` (bound by `main.ts`). An order no row holds is acknowledged, written nowhere and reported.
- FR-371, R-221 (Phase 2, T056; the owner's decision of 2026-11-04): a paid event whose amount or currency is not its order's, compared under the order's lock before anything is recorded, writes nothing, is reported (the gateway's order and transaction ids, the two amounts and currencies, no personal data) so it can be refunded from the gateway's dashboard, and is refused with `VALIDATION_FAILED`: `order-events.service.ts`. The adapter itself is the port's (`paymob.adapter.ts`).
- FR-351 to FR-357, FR-BOOK-12 (Phase 2, T036; R-208, ARCHITECTURE §8.1): `checkout.service.ts` and `checkout.router.ts`, the student's `checkout.hold`, `checkout.pay` and `checkout.retry`. Keyed in two steps (the owner's decision of 2026-11-03): the key read first, a completed one answered from its outcome with no gateway call; the gateway's order created outside any transaction and recorded under the key, the order a duplicate made unnecessary or the transaction refused cancelled at the gateway; the stored outcome holds no address, read again from `checkout_url_encrypted` while the order is `created` and unexpired. Booking's holds and enrollments are written through its entry (`placeCheckoutHold`, `payTargetOf`, `enrollOnHold`, `lockOrderTarget`).
- FR-380 to FR-386, FR-BOOK-12 (c) (Phase 2, T037; data-model §2.4.1): `checkout.current`, `checkout.continue` and `checkout.status` on the same router. `current` answers the student's own unexpired hold, its package's summary now and its last order's `canContinue`, decrypting nothing; `continue` answers `NOT_FOUND` unless the enrollment is the caller's and decrypts the last order's address only while it is `created` and before its `expires_at` (`ORDER_NOT_PAYABLE` otherwise); `status` answers `NOT_FOUND` unless the enrollment is the caller's, with its last order's gateway reference. The logger's redaction list covers `checkoutUrl`, `checkoutUri` and `checkoutLink`.
- FR-400 to FR-402, FR-PAYG-11, FR-ADMIN-18 (Phase 2, T052; R-220): the admin's read-only monitor, `payments-admin.router.ts` (`payments.list`, `payments.get`, mounted by `admin.router.ts`, composed into the admin module's services), `payments-monitor.service.ts` and `payments-monitor.repository.ts`. Newest first by the time-ordered id, keyset pages of `admin_list_page_size`; a search matches an order reference exactly or a student's folded name by prefix (R-203), scanning windows that double until the page is full. The detail: the amounts as reported (fee and net once paid), the references, the enrollment's package and progress through booking's entry, the order's events, its outcomes, and every paid order of the enrollment (the note under T052). Each call writes its one audit entry, `payments.listed` naming the reader or `payments.viewed` naming the order (`payment`), and nothing else (FR-401).
