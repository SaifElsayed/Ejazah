## How to read this document

This version exists because v1.0, while a good product description, was not buildable without invention: ten requirements directly contradicted other requirements, and roughly forty decisions an engineer hits in week three were unstated. Every one of those is now resolved.

Three markers appear throughout:

| Marker    | Meaning                                                                                               |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [DEC-nn]  | A decision made in the v1.0 → v1.1 review. Listed in full in §14.                                     |
| [RISK-nn] | An accepted risk. The team has chosen to carry it knowingly. Listed in §15.                           |
| [OPEN-nn] | Genuinely unresolved. Blocks nothing today but must close before the stated milestone. Listed in §16. |

The rule for the team: if you are writing code and this document does not tell you what to do, stop and raise an OPEN item. Do not decide it at the keyboard. That is the failure mode this rewrite exists to prevent.

Requirements use `FR-<MODULE>-<n>` and `NFR-<CATEGORY>-<n>`. "Shall" denotes mandatory, testable MVP behaviour. IDs from v1.0 are preserved where the requirement survived, so old tickets and notes still resolve.

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Product Overview

Ijazah is a two-sided marketplace mobile application pairing students seeking verified instruction in the Qur'an and Islamic sciences (fiqh, aqeedah, hadith, tajweed and related disciplines) with admin-vetted teachers, for live one-to-one online sessions. The product is organised around a single outcome: the ijazah, a formal certificate of authorisation granted by a teacher to a student who has demonstrated mastery under that teacher's direct supervision. The platform digitises scheduling, payment, delivery and certification of that relationship while preserving its trust, rigour and cultural norms.

### 1.2 Business Objectives

- Become the trusted marketplace for verified one-to-one Qur'an and Islamic-sciences instruction.
- Digitise the ijazah-granting relationship without diluting its authenticity.
- Give teachers reliable income and low-friction reach: every booking is paid through the gateway before a session is generated, and the net settles straight to the teacher *(v1.4)*.
- Give students confidence that every teacher is vetted and that gender-appropriate interaction is enforced by the product, not negotiated informally.
- Validate demand in Egypt/MENA before monetising or expanding scope.

### 1.3 Definitions

| Term                 | Definition                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ijazah               | Traditional certificate of authorisation granted by a teacher to a student, certifying mastery of a text or subject.               |
| Isnad                | Chain of transmission linking a teacher back through their own teachers. Not in MVP (§13).                                         |
| Teacher              | A user approved by an admin to offer paid one-to-one sessions and grant ijazahs.                                                   |
| Student              | A user who books, pays through the gateway for, and attends sessions.                                                              |

| Escrow               | *Retired.* The platform never holds funds; the gateway settles directly to the Teacher (§3.6).                                       |

| Admin                | Internal operator with access to the web admin panel.                                                                              |

## 2. Overall Description

### 2.1 Product Perspective

Three components:

- **Mobile application** (iOS and Android, single codebase; the first store release is Android only, iOS following at the first App Store submission *(amended 2026-11-10, DEC-62)*) — used by students and teachers for discovery, booking, payment, live sessions, chat, certification and reviews.
- **Admin web panel** — desktop web, internal operators only, Arabic and English interface on the app's design system [DEC-31, DEC-54].
- **Backend platform** — authentication, scheduling, gateway checkout and webhook confirmation, live-session orchestration, notifications, storage.

### 2.2 One Role per Account [DEC-30, DEC-56]

*(Rewritten 2026-10-15, [ADR 0016](adr/0016-one-role-per-account-and-no-paper-copy-flag.md).)* An account holds exactly one role, Student or Teacher, chosen at the first sign-in (FR-AUTH-2, FR-AUTH-12). The role cannot be changed afterwards and no second role can be added. A teacher who wishes to study uses a separate account under another email. Dual-role accounts are deferred (§13).

Consequences, which are binding on the data model and every authorisation check:

- The app shows the navigation of the account's one role. There is no role switcher.
- Gender rules apply to the account's role: a female Teacher is discoverable only by female Students, and a female Student sees and books only female Teachers (§3.7.1).
- A user shall never be able to enroll with themselves. Enforced server-side.
- Suspension acts on the account (`users.account_status`); withdrawing a Teacher's approval acts on the Teacher profile (FR-VERIFY-8). There is no per-role suspension.

### 2.3 User Classes

| Class   | Description                                                                       | Proficiency                                   |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Student | Books, pays for and attends sessions; receives ijazahs; leaves reviews.           | Low–medium; must be simple and mobile-first.  |
| Teacher | Vetted expert. Sets availability and packages, conducts sessions, grants ijazahs. | Low–medium; many more comfortable in Arabic.  |
| Admin   | Internal operator: vetting, disputes, oversight.                         | Medium–high; data-dense dashboard acceptable. |

### 2.4 Operating Environment

- Mobile: iOS and Android smartphones, single cross-platform codebase. The first store release runs on Android only; iOS follows at the first App Store submission *(amended 2026-11-10, DEC-62)*.
- Admin: modern desktop browsers, latest two major versions.
- Backend: cloud-hosted over HTTPS, with a managed third-party video provider.
- Network: MENA mobile data and home broadband; usable on 3G/4G with instability.

### 2.5 Constraints

- The MVP is built by a team of one (PLAN.md, "Schedule and working method", revised 2026-10-08): thirty working days after Phase 0 for Phases 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6, each phase with its own Spec Kit cycle, batches of at most six tasks, one pull request and one review per batch. *(Amended 2026-11-10, DEC-62: the dated road in `PLAN.md`, «The road to the store», replaces those days' dates: the closed test by 2026-12-06 and the store by 2027-01-01, with the programme of §3.5.1 built before the server.)* (v1.0 to v1.4 said "a small team (2 full-time, 1 part-time) in 4 months"; corrected in the Phase 1 document pass, R-PROC-2.)
- The platform is a technology intermediary, never a custodian. Payment flows Student → gateway → Teacher's payout account, with 0% platform commission, no wallet, no escrow and no ledger (§3.6, ADR 0007). Gateway selection is OPEN-11 *(v1.4)*. *(Amended 2026-11-17, ADR 0025: the platform takes a commission, the Teacher's share net of every cost; the gateway holds the Teacher's share until released at milestones; still no wallet, escrow or ledger, and the platform holds nothing.)*
- Gender-interaction rules (§3.7) are system-enforced hard rules, not guidance.
- Currency is EGP, stored as integer piasters [DEC-13].

### 2.6 Assumptions and Dependencies

- Users self-report gender at signup; trusted until disputed, at which point an admin reviews (§3.7.4).
- Users self-report adulthood. No hard age block [DEC-04, RISK-01].
- Teachers have a device with camera, microphone and a connection adequate for live video.
- The chosen video provider offers server-minted, per-participant session tokens and session-lifecycle webhooks. Per-participant _publish_ permissions are **not** required: that constraint was retired with the camera matrix in §3.7.2 and no longer governs provider selection.

### 3.6 Payments

#### 3.6.0 One payment model — direct gateway checkout [ADR 0007, DEC-49]

The Student pays through a payment gateway at checkout. The gateway deducts its own fee and settles the net — 100% of it — directly to the Teacher's payout account. The platform takes **0% commission**, holds no funds at any moment, keeps no wallet, ledger, balance or escrow, and never touches money in transit. Its role is to create the gateway order, verify the gateway's signed webhook, and turn a confirmed payment into a confirmed Enrollment with generated sessions. *(Amended 2026-11-17, ADR 0025:)* the gateway settles the Teacher's share of the gross, a rate an Administrator sets (§3.14, default 70%), to the Teacher's payout account, held until released at milestones (DEC-81); the platform's part, the rest, bears the gateway's fee and every other cost. The platform still holds no funds and keeps no wallet, ledger, balance or escrow.

Everything after confirmation is independent of money inside the platform: attendance evaluation, the no-show state machine, cancellation, certification and reviews carry no monetary consequence in code. Where a termination leaves paid-for sessions undelivered, reimbursement is an administrative matter between the Student and Platform Administration, decided through the moderation workflow (FR-DISPUTE-5/6) and executed outside the platform's code path. *(Amended 2026-11-17, ADR 0025: a missed session is remedied by a make-up only (DEC-84); a refund an Administrator orders when an Enrollment is closed is computed by the platform (DEC-85, DEC-86).)*

### 3.7 Live Sessions and Gender-Interaction Rules

The most sensitive area of the product. Gender-appropriate interaction is a hard, system-enforced rule at every layer — discovery, booking, video, screen share and chat.

#### 3.7.1 Discovery and Pairing Rule [DEC-39]

Cross-gender pairings do not exist on this platform, in any direction, under any circumstance.

- A Male Student sees, searches, books and meets only Male Teachers.
- A Female Student sees, searches, books and meets only Female Teachers.

Enforced at the data layer, not the UI layer. Teachers of the opposite gender are excluded from the result set of any query executed on behalf of a Student, at the API and database level — so the restriction cannot be bypassed by inspecting network traffic, guessing a profile ID, or any client-side manipulation. The profile is absent, not greyed out or locked. A direct request for such a profile returns the same response as a request for a non-existent profile.

The rule is symmetric, which makes it simpler to reason about and simpler to test than the asymmetric v1.1 rule it replaces: there is exactly one legal pairing — same gender.

**Visibility predicate** *(v1.3, ADR 0006)*. A Teacher is visible to a requester on any Student-facing path — search, browse, direct profile access, booking, token issuance — if and only if all five hold:

```
teacher.gender = requester.gender
AND teacher_profile.verification_status = 'approved'      (FR-DISC-5)
AND teacher.deleted_at IS NULL
AND teacher_profile.payout_verified_at IS NOT NULL                (FR-PAYG-14, v1.4)
AND teacher.account_status NOT IN ('suspended', 'banned')         (2026-10-31)
```

Booking and token issuance further require `teacher.account_status = 'active'`: a Teacher whose account is Verification-Required stays visible, as FR-VERIFY-10 restricts the account's new bookings and not its existence, but cannot be booked until the account is active again *(2026-10-31, Phase 2's Spec Kit cycle; the owner's decision of 2026-10-29, task T143 of `specs/002-identity/tasks.md`)*.

The predicate is implemented once, as the repository filter every Teacher-returning query on a Student-facing path must take (Constitution R-GENDER-5), and is enforced identically in both directions: a Male requester never receives Female Teacher data and a Female requester never receives Male Teacher data (NFR-SEC-4). Counts and facets are computed after the predicate is applied (R-GENDER-3).

**Open gender dispute** *(v1.3, ADR 0006, OPEN-09)*. While a `gender_flags` row is pending, the flagged user remains active with their declared gender and the predicate above is applied to that declared value. No pre-emptive exclusion, hiding or suspension occurs. Only an Admin ruling changes the record (FR-GENDER-4), with the consequences FR-GENDER-6 and FR-GENDER-7 define.

> **Business consequence, stated once:** this halves effective liquidity on both sides. Supply must be grown in both genders in parallel; a pool of excellent male teachers is worth nothing to a female student. Factor this into launch-cohort recruitment.

| Female Student — Female Teacher | Optional, user's own choice   | Optional, user's own choice   |

Publishing video is always optional — nobody is ever required to turn a camera on, and either party may disable their camera at any time.

Before a participant shares their whole screen, the app shall warn them, every time and in both roles, before the operating system's own permission prompt, that the other party will see everything on the screen, notifications included, with a choice to continue or cancel; a cancel sends nothing and starts no share *(added 2026-11-08, the owner's decision)*.

Nothing about camera state is evaluated, snapshotted or persisted against a Session. There is no camera rule to resolve at session start and no per-participant publish permission to mint into a token — FR-SESS-3 binds a token to one session and one participant, and nothing further. An implementer needing a camera decision at any layer should read this subsection as complete.

> **What this removes from v1.1:** the per-participant publish-permission matrix, the female-student camera lockout, the screen-share bypass rule, the per-session `camera_rule_snapshot` field now retired from §6.2, and — importantly — the hard requirement that the video provider support server-controlled per-participant publish permissions. That constraint no longer governs provider selection. Session tokens are still minted server-side, for authentication and session binding.

Rebuilt from v1.0. **AdminRecoveryCode** — id, admin_id, code_hash, used_at. Ten single-use codes per administrator (ADR 0019 D3).

**SessionRemedy** — id, session_id (unique), kind (Makeup / Refund), status (Awaiting-Choice / Proposed / Counter-Proposed / Booked / Refund-Case), proposed_date, counter_date, decided_at, case_id. What a session not consumed is owed (ADR 0018); columns only in Phase 1, written by Phase 3.

Remaining entities (Review, Dispute, GenderFlag, UserReport, ChatMessage, Notification, Subject, TeacherSubject, VerificationDocument, VerificationRequest — with document_id and document_type since the Phase 1 pass —, OtpCode — with the Account-Deletion purpose —, DevicePushToken, TermsAcceptance, IjazahBar, EnrollmentEvent — since the Phase 2 pass —) follow the same conventions and are detailed in the accompanying ERD.

## 7. Business Rules Summary

**Gender.** Every session is same-gender, without exception. Male students see and meet only male teachers; female students only female teachers. Enforced server-side at the query layer. There is consequently no gender-based camera restriction — camera is optional for both parties in every session. No images in chat. Gender is self-declared, changeable only via Admin review; proven falsification is grounds for ban *(v1.3)*.

**Money.** The platform is a technology intermediary, never a custodian. The Student pays the gateway; the gateway deducts its fee and settles 100% of the net directly to the Teacher's verified payout account; the platform takes 0% commission and holds nothing. Payment is confirmed only by the gateway's signed webhook, which confirms the Enrollment and generates sessions automatically. No wallet, no ledger, no balance, no manual paid flag. Reimbursement is an Admin decision through the moderation workflow, executed by Platform Administration outside the platform's code path, never a negotiation between Student and Teacher *(v1.4)*. *(Amended 2026-11-17, ADR 0025:)* the platform takes a commission; the Teacher's share, net of every cost, is held by the gateway until released at milestones; a missed session is remedied by a make-up only; a closed Enrollment is refunded by its cause's rule. The platform is still never a custodian and holds nothing.

**Attendance.** Student absent, Teacher present → session consumed. Teacher absent, Student present → session not consumed, no-show counted, and the Student is owed a make-up session or, on recitation, a refund. Both absent → treated as mutual cancellation: not consumed, no penalty either side, a make-up session follows *(ADR 0018)*. Below attendance threshold or provider outage → Admin review. Teacher no-shows never end an Enrollment by themselves: at the repeated-absence flag count the Enrollment is marked for Administrators, and nothing else follows. The Student may complain of the Teacher's absence; only an Administrator ends the Enrollment → remaining sessions cancelled, the Enrollment terminated, and a compensation case opened for the Admin (FR-DISPUTE-5) *(v1.4)*; an Enrollment so ended may be restored within the restore window (FR-CANCEL-8) *(amended 2026-11-12, DEC-69: restorable at any time while the closing's case is open; the window only holds the slot, FR-CANCEL-9)* *(2026-11-05, the owner's decision, ADR 0023: was «Teacher no-shows past threshold → remaining sessions cancelled, the Enrollment terminated»)*.

**Cancellation.** Symmetric. Either party may request; only the counterparty's acceptance makes it penalty-free. Decline or silence means the session stands and ordinary attendance rules apply. No enrollment-level cancellation, no rescheduling in MVP *(except a one-week postponement by either party, §3.8.6, DEC-86)*.

**Certification.** A Teacher's own ijazah is a PDF per subject; the Admin approves it and transcribes its chain once. A Student's ijazah is issued by the Admin on the Teacher's request, as a platform-generated certificate with the Teacher's signature and the chain, never on the Admin's own initiative *(ADR 0017)*. Chains have no maximum length and are snapshotted onto each certificate at issue. Admins may revoke or bar. Revocation does not bar a student from future ijazahs. Certificates are publicly verifiable and never hard-deleted.

**Trust.** No Teacher is discoverable until approved via document review and a recorded verification call, and until the gateway has verified their payout account (§3.6.2). A Student may review after any terminal Enrollment where at least one session occurred.

**An account's end** *(2026-11-12, DEC-66, DEC-67)*. Any ban, and a Teacher's revocation, can be lifted at any time, and the lift restores the Enrollments it ended, their slots held for 14 days (a setting) and afterwards restored while still free. Any Enrollment end an Administrator started can be restored the same way; an Administrator may end an Enrollment directly, with a reason both parties are shown (DEC-68, DEC-69). A Teacher's suspension pauses their Enrollments. An account with an ongoing Enrollment cannot be deleted; a Teacher once approved keeps, after deletion, their identity, credential and payout documents for fraud prevention, until an Administrator erases them.

## 9. Key Use Cases

**UC-1 — Teacher application and approval.** Applicant uploads ID and, per subject chosen, the ijazah they hold as a PDF *(ADR 0017)* → appears in Admin queue as Pending → Admin reviews documents, approves each ijazah PDF and transcribes its chain (FR-ISNAD-2), conducts an out-of-app verification call, records the outcome → Approve becomes available → Admin approves → Teacher notified, profile and payout setup unlocked → Teacher registers a payout account with the gateway in-app → gateway verifies it → packages and availability publishable, Teacher becomes discoverable subject to gender rules *(v1.4)*. Alternates: rejection with reason (may reapply); more-information request (stays Pending with a visible note); ban (no further applications).

**UC-2 — Student discovers and books.** Student searches or browses → server returns only gender-compatible approved Teachers → opens profile, selects Package → chooses a recurring slot of matching duration → slot held for the checkout TTL plus the hold grace (FR-BOOK-9) → Student reviews the order on the Checkout screen and taps Pay → Enrollment created as Pending-Payment, linked to the hold, and a gateway order created → pays on the gateway's hosted page → app shows "Confirming your payment" → gateway's signed webhook reports paid → confirm-enrollment job sets Confirmed, converts the hold, generates all sessions at the recurring slot → app renders "Payment successful" with the receipt and sessions from server state (FR-PAYG-17) → both parties notified; net proceeds settle to the Teacher's payout account *(v1.4)*. Alternates: order fails or is cancelled at the gateway → hold kept, Student may Retry on it or choose another slot (FR-PAYG-18, FR-BOOK-12); hold expires → hold released, Enrollment cancelled, Student may start again (FR-PAYG-6); slot taken during checkout → prompted to choose another; paid after the hold was released and the slot or a party's time taken meanwhile → Enrollment cancelled as `slot_unavailable` and a compensation case opened (FR-PAYG-19); replayed webhook → acknowledged, no effect *(ADR 0010)*.

**UC-3 — Live session.** Both tap Join within the early-join window → server verifies from its own records that both participants are the same gender and refuses to issue tokens otherwise (FR-SESS-2) → server mints each participant a token bound to that session and participant, carrying no publish restriction (FR-SESS-3) → the pre-session screen confirms the counterparty, the duration and each participant's own camera and microphone state (FR-SESS-5) → either party optionally enables their camera, and may disable it again at any time (§3.7.2) → participants converse; text and PDF chat permitted, images rejected → after scheduled end, server evaluates simultaneous connected time → ≥ threshold → Done, session consumed *(v1.3)*. Alternates: below threshold → Admin review queue; provider outage → Admin review, no penalty. There is no mixed-gender variant of this case: cross-gender pairings cannot occur (§3.7.1).

**UC-4 — Teacher no-show, the Student's complaint and the Administrator's decision.** Student joins, Teacher does not within the join window → session marked Teacher-No-Show after scheduled end → session not consumed → the Student is owed a make-up session or, on recitation, a refund (FR-NOSHOW-2), and the Enrollment continues → no-show count (derived) rises; at the repeated-absence flag count the Enrollment is marked for Administrators, and nothing happens automatically (FR-NOSHOW-3) → the Student may complain of the Teacher's absence, with a written reason; one complaint open at a time (FR-NOSHOW-4) → the Administrator ends the Enrollment (remaining sessions cancelled, Enrollment set Terminated-No-Show, both parties notified; the Student remains eligible to review, FR-REVIEW-1, and a compensation case opens in the Admin moderation queue), suspends the Teacher, or closes the complaint with a note → within the restore window the Administrator may restore an Enrollment so ended (FR-CANCEL-8). *(amended 2026-11-12, DEC-69: restorable at any time while the closing's case is open; the window only holds the slot, FR-CANCEL-9)* Reimbursement is the Admin's decision (FR-DISPUTE-5/6) *(v1.4; rewritten 2026-11-05, the owner's decision, ADR 0023: was «at threshold, remaining sessions cancelled and Enrollment set Terminated-No-Show»)*.

**UC-5 — Teacher requests cancellation.** Teacher submits a request with reason → Student notified → Accept → session Cancelled-By-Agreement, not consumed, no no-show *(v1.3)*. Decline or no response → session stands; if Teacher then misses it, ordinary no-show rules apply in full.

**UC-6 — Dispute resolution.** Party raises a dispute within the dispute window → session outcome frozen as Disputed → dispute appears in Admin queue with attendance telemetry and chat log → Admin reviews and records a binding resolution with reason (outcome upheld or re-marked, no-show attributed, compensation ordered, or a party suspended or banned) → both parties notified → audit entry written → any ordered compensation executed by Platform Administration through the gateway, recorded when the gateway reports it. No funds move in the platform's code path *(v1.4)*.

**UC-7 — Requesting and issuing an ijazah.** *(Rewritten, ADR 0017.)* Teacher opens the Enrollment, selects Request Ijazah → enters the completion date and certificate language → sees the filled certificate → sends → system checks the Teacher's approved credential for the subject, the signature, and that the Student is not barred → request appears in the Admin queue with the same filled certificate → Admin approves → system generates the PDF from the template with unique number, QR and verification token, records it on the Student's profile, notifies both. Alternates: Admin rejects with a reason → Teacher notified; Student barred → request refused; erroneous issue → Admin revokes with reason, record retained.

**UC-8 — Retired (v1.3, ADR 0005).** Was withdrawal.

## 11. Assumptions, Dependencies, Constraints

**Assumptions.** Users self-report adulthood; DOB is collected but not enforced [RISK-01]. Gender is binary for discovery and pairing, self-declared, correctable only via Admin review; it does not drive camera logic (§3.7.2). Teachers have adequate devices and connectivity. Initial market is Egypt/MENA; currency EGP, Arabic first. The platform takes no commission and never holds money *(v1.4)*. A single admin team suffices for MVP volume — and in MVP that team is the three founders, which is real capacity consumed, not free.

**Dependencies.** Video provider API stability, server-minted session tokens and lifecycle webhooks (critical to §3.7); per-participant publish control is no longer required (§3.7.2). Payment gateway availability, individual-recipient onboarding and signed webhooks (critical to §3.6, OPEN-11). Admin capacity to conduct verification calls promptly, since this gates teacher earning. Apple Developer and Google Play account approval, which can take weeks and should be started immediately.

**Constraints.** Team of 2 full-time and 1 part-time, 4 months, no prior mobile/video/payments experience. Anything not explicitly specified here is out of scope until formally added. Gender rules are non-negotiable product requirements, not adjustable preferences.
