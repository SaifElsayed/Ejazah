# Open items from the screen inventory

What the inventory (`screens.md`) and the copy catalogue found undrawn, ambiguous or in need of the
owner's decision, by area, as recorded on 2026-10-17. Each item is a decision for the owner, a state
the implementer adds from the generic patterns, or a copy variant the catalogue completed beyond what
one board draws (marked as such). None of them blocks implementing what is drawn.

Closed on 2026-10-17: every item ends with its disposition — **drawn** (a board exists; `screens.md`
names it), **in the decision sheet** (`decisions-2026-10.md`, the row number), **in the copy review**
(`copy-review-2026-10.md`, the section), **ADR 0021** (the data or configuration consequence, with
its decision), or **closed** with the reason. The decision sheet references the items here by area
(A1, A2, B, C) and number.

## Catalogue conventions worth knowing

37. Package naming uses one ICU key with nested plurals (`common.package.full` «{subject} · {sessions, plural, …} · {minutes, plural, …}»), never fragments. `common.unit.sessions` / `minutes` exist for stand-alone counts. → **closed: convention**
38. Weekday names in «كل {weekday} {time}» are used in their bare form («أربعاء», «سبت») while dates use the article («الأربعاء 4 نوفمبر»); the date formatter must supply both forms. → **closed: convention**
