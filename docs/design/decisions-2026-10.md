# Product decisions from the screen inventory — the record, October 2026

What the inventory of 2026-10-17 (`screens.md`, `open-items.md`) found that the design did not
settle. On 2026-10-17 the owner asked for everything to be resolved before implementation starts
(«قم بحل الباقي، أريد أن أنهي كل شيء حتى أبدأ في التنفيذ»), so every row below was decided by its
recommendation (best practice, the SRS and the ADRs, the design's own conventions) and applied in the
same commit: to the boards (`source/frags/`), to `source/BRIEF-PROD.md`, to the catalogues, to
`screens.md` (the rules are its section "Rules decided on 2026-10-17") and to `open-items.md`. Each
row is marked **قرار مُطبَّق** with the reason and where it landed. The owner can reverse any row; the
ones taken with real hesitation are listed at the end.

## Rows taken with real hesitation (the owner may want to reverse these)

- **19 — the status words.** BRIEF-PROD's «لم يحضر أحد (لا تُحتسب)» and «قيد المراجعة» were the owner's
  earlier words; the boards' words won because FACTS (2026-10-17) already lists them as the phone
  vocabulary. Reversing is two badge strings.
- **28 — optional note on approvals.** The drawn contract made every note mandatory. Reversing is one
  label, one footer and `disabled` on one button.
- **38 — the proposals removed outright.** The alternative was to keep the two teacher proposals
  for a later round. Their fragments are under `source/proposals/`; restoring them is a relayout.
- **46 — date of birth locked on the student's profile.** The brief asked for editing; it is locked
  because the minor flag follows it. Unlocking is one attribute on the board and one hint.
- **17 — the Terms as served content.** Six catalogue groups were dropped; if the owner prefers app
  copy, they come back from the previous commit.
- **26 — the public pages on the API's origin.** A separate static site is the usual choice; the
  API's origin was chosen to avoid a deploy and paid hosting before launch.

**The owner's review (2026-10-17).** All six rows are confirmed as applied. Row 17 and row 26 carry
conditions, written into ADR 0021 D5: the Terms and the public pages are served by the API under
their own paths, with a strict CSP, no cookie, escaping templates and the anonymous rate limits; the
Terms carry the version FR-AUTH-11 records. Row 46 stays locked because the date of birth drives the
minor flag; a change goes through the admin. ADR 0021 D1–D4 are accepted the same day.

The audit target labels, the English subject names, the notification kinds FR-NOTIF-1 lists and
the design does not draw, and the support address are data-model and configuration decisions: ADR
0021 D1–D4 (proposed). The Discovery search and filters and the public pages' home are ADR 0021 D5
and D6 (decided).
